About

Built from a fishing boat, not a business plan.

Captain Matthew has commercially fished the waters of Tampa Bay for over 20 years. Hookin' Ain't Easy started as a way to sell that catch directly — it's grown into a full seafood market, a dockside cafe, an outdoor patio, and a food truck, with live music most weekends. Matthew and Veronica run it together, and most of what's in the case, Matthew caught himself.
